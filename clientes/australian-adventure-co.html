Blue Mountains
Private Tour

The Three Sisters, Wentworth Falls, spectacular lookouts, and charming mountain villages — you will see the best of the Blue Mountains. How the day unfolds is up to you. The route, the pace, and the hidden extras are shaped entirely around your group, guided by someone who knows every trail and every secret viewpoint.

How Your Day Unfolds

A Day in the Blue Mountains

AM
Morning · Pickup & Departure

Sydney to the Mountains

We collect you from your hotel or accommodation and head west into the Blue Mountains, arriving in time to beat the crowds at the most spectacular lookouts. An optional stop at Featherdale Wildlife Park en route is available at your discretion.

Mid
Midday · Exploration

Lookouts, Trails & Villages

The Three Sisters at Echo Point, rainforest walks to whichever waterfall best suits the day, and hidden lookouts most visitors never find. Coffee or lunch in Leura or a lesser-known mountain village.

PM
Afternoon · Return

Unhurried Return to Sydney

We take the scenic route home, stopping where the light and the mood call for it. Back in Sydney by early evening, tired in the best possible way.

Ancient Landscapes

Lookouts
Above the Clouds

Quiet vantage points away from the crowds, where ancient sandstone escarpments drop into valleys of blue haze. We take you to the lookouts most visitors never find — panoramic, unhurried, shared only with your group. Including the iconic Three Sisters from Echo Point, where Aboriginal legend meets breathtaking geology.

Into the Wilderness

Hidden
Waterfalls

Rainforest trails that lead through sparkling cascades to waterfalls dropping into deep sandstone canyons. Wentworth Falls, the Valley of the Waters, or a quieter track your guide knows — the route depends on the day, the season, and what feels right.

Charming Villages

Mountain Towns &
Local Flavour

Coffee in Leura, lunch at a restaurant in a lesser-known town, browsing boutique shops in a village that time forgot. The mountains are as much about these unhurried moments as the dramatic scenery — and your guide knows exactly where to go.

Optional Add-Ons

Enhance Your Day

These experiences can be added to your tour and are not included in the standard tour price.

Featherdale Wildlife Park

Hand-feed kangaroos and wallabies. Meet koalas, wombats, echidnas, quokkas, and bilbies — a gentle introduction to Australia's native wildlife.

Tickets can be arranged in advance — just let us know when booking

Scenic World

Ride the world's steepest railway into ancient Jurassic rainforest deep in the Jamison Valley. Skyway, cableway, and walkway experiences available. Allow approximately 2 hours.

Tickets can be arranged in advance — just let us know when booking

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Approximately 8–9 hours from pickup to drop-off. It's designed as a full-day immersive adventure so you can enjoy the region at a relaxed, unhurried pace.
Included is your luxury private vehicle, hotel pickup and drop-off, a fully tailored itinerary, expert local guide, bottled water throughout, and all national park entry fees. Lunch, Featherdale Wildlife Park entry, and Scenic World entry are not included and are available as optional add-ons.
A typical day includes an optional stop at Featherdale Wildlife Park en route, panoramic lookouts including Lincoln's Rock and Anvil Rock, rainforest walks to waterfalls, the iconic Three Sisters at Echo Point, coffee or lunch in Leura or a mountain village, and an optional Scenic World visit. The order and pace is entirely flexible and shaped around your group.
From $1,200 AUD for two guests — solo travellers are also welcome. The price covers your entire private group for the full day.
Yes — pickup and drop-off from hotels or cruise terminals in central Sydney is included. Just let us know your location when booking and we'll coordinate the timing.
Tickets for Scenic World and Featherdale Wildlife Park are not included in the tour price but can be arranged in advance — just let us know when booking and we'll coordinate everything for you.
We can adjust the itinerary to focus on sheltered or scenic stops that still deliver a great experience. We're also happy to reschedule or provide a refund if notified the day before departure.
Comfortable walking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing (it can be noticeably cooler in the mountains than Sydney), sunscreen, a hat, and a camera. Layers are useful as mountain weather can change quickly throughout the day.
